Neil, Here is design 3 4 2^13 in 24 runs.  
It was a bit of a struggle to generate.  
My usual method of looking for 3 two-level factors to replace with
a 4 (in 3 2^16) did not work since there is not a 2 that is the
perfect interaction of two other 2's.
While I still have a long ways to go, my new software can generate 
most of the designs in Table 12.7 of Hedayat, Sloane, and Stufken,
and a lot of other designs too.
